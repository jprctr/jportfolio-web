# Kleptocrat - The Mintz Group

## Project Details

### Title
Kleptocrat

### Brief
Client request: Create a digital card game for iOS in the public interest that teaches players about kleptocracy and money laundering. The game should analyze 35 famous historical cases of kleptocracy, categorizing the techniques and breaking them down into clear steps to create cards illustrating these real-world methods. The gameplay lets players take on the role of a corrupt politician, moving their money, covering their tracks, choosing associates to trust, and living large - all while learning about risk factors at play. The game aims to help law enforcement, journalists, and NGOs trace and recover misappropriated public assets, while alerting citizens to asset-hiding patterns.

## Consolidated Narrative

Following the release of Panama Papers in 2016, I worked with the investigative firm The Mintz Group and developers BumbleBear Games to create Kleptocrat, a digital card game for iOS. The game analyzes 35 famous historical cases of kleptocracy, categorizing the techniques, breaking them down into clear steps, and creating cards to illustrate these real-world methods.

I was responsible for developing Kleptocrat as a digital card game for iOS. For this project, I learned C# and Unity by collaborating closely with a developer from the team at BumbleBear Games. We worked together to create an inviting interface and sticky gameplay loop without losing sight of the content and the project's mission.

The game's primary audience included the general public, journalists, and NGOs. By letting players play as the corrupt politician, the game aims to help them recognize and bust corruption and money laundering. The game was designed to help law enforcement, journalists, and NGOs trace and recover misappropriated public assets.

Our design process was highly iterative, beginning with paper prototypes with both gameplay and investigative experts in the room. We collaborated with experts to ensure game mechanics accurately reflected real-world corruption patterns and aligned with the project's educational mission. We created an inviting interface that made the serious subject matter accessible and engaging, and designed gameplay that allowed players to experience corruption tactics first-hand while learning about detection and prevention.

From a technical standpoint, I needed to learn C# and Unity while maintaining the project schedule and translate complex investigative concepts into engaging game mechanics. I collaborated closely with a developer from BumbleBear and the team to pick up C# and Unity, then iterated extensively on game mechanics with feedback from investigative experts. I designed card-based gameplay that naturally mapped to the corruption investigation process and focused on creating an engaging iOS experience that was both fun and educational.

The final deliverable was a unique game of strategy and tactics, based on patterns of money laundering and offshore structuring that have been used by corrupt public officials. The game lets players take on the role of a corrupt politician, moving their money, covering their tracks, choosing associates to trust, and living large - all while learning about the risk factors at play. The game breaks down the techniques of kleptocracy into clear steps, helping players understand how to take, hide, and move dirty money, then engage co-conspirators or abuse power to try to cover up actions and enjoy ill-gotten gains without getting caught.

This game offers insight into the strategies of the corrupt, as well as those who are trying to bring them to justice. It's a unique educational tool that combines engaging gameplay with serious investigative journalism to raise awareness about kleptocracy and money laundering while empowering players with knowledge to identify and combat these practices.
