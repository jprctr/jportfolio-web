# You Watch Party Interactive Experience - Cosmopolitan Magazine

## Project Details

### Title
You Watch Party Interactive Experience

### Brief
Client request: Build a second screen interactive mobile companion experience for the season two premiere of Netflix's You, created in collaboration with Cosmopolitan Magazine. The experience needed to feature behind-the-scenes content, quizzes, and videos about each episode that played out in real time along with the episode being watched. A CMS was needed to enable the Cosmopolitan team to upload content that would be generated into custom interactive cards playing out sequentially in real time. At the end of each episode, users should be able to view a quiz results report and share it with friends.

## Consolidated Narrative

As part of the team at Pitch Interactive, I built out the front end of the You Watch Party Interactive Experience for the season two premiere of Netflix's "You." This second screen interactive mobile experience was created in collaboration with Cosmopolitan Magazine's Entertainment Editor and Netflix.

My colleague created a backend CMS in Python running on Google App Engine, which was populated with content that played out in real time along with each episode created by our clients, the team at Cosmopolitan Magazine. I was responsible for building a responsive interface designed for optimal mobile experience, with "cards" timed to appear alongside key moments in each episode. These cards featured interactive or multimedia elements including behind-the-scenes trivia, quizzes, and videos. I implemented real-time content synchronization with the episode playback using requestAnimationFrame in a custom timer component for precise timing control and smooth pause and resume functionality.

The primary audience was Netflix viewers watching You season 2 premiere. Our goal was to create an engaging companion experience that enhanced their viewing without distracting from the show.

Our design process focused on creating a mobile-first web application that would responsively resize to a variety of device sizes while maintaining a straightforward and clean UI. We tested our time-aligned content with pre-release cuts of each episode, which were fine-tuned by the Cosmopolitan team when the season was ready to drop. This ensured that the content appeared at the right moments in the episode and that the timing was perfectly synchronized.

On the technical side, we needed to aligning content with selected moments in each episode while allowing users to pause and resume. We created a custom timer component using requestAnimationFrame for precise timing control, implemented real-time content delivery synchronized with episode playback, and created seamless pause and resume functionality. We also needed to ensure smooth animations and interactions for a second-screen experience.

The final deliverable was a compelling second screen interactive mobile experience that enhanced the viewing of Netflix's You season 2 premiere. The web app featured behind-the-scenes content, quizzes, and videos about each episode created by the Cosmopolitan team, all playing out in real time along with the companion episode. At the end of each episode, users could view a report on how well they answered the quizzes, prompting them to share their results with friends before continuing onto the next episode. This experience successfully engaged viewers in a deeper exploration of the show's themes and content while they watched.
