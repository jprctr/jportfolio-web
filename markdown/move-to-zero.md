# Move to Zero: Climate and Sport - Nike

## Project Details

### Title
Move to Zero: Climate and Sport

### Brief
Client request: Create an narrative data visualization experience for the 2019 Climate Summit in NYC about current climate conditions and their impact on sports around the world. The project, in collaboration with Rhodium Group, needed to focus on five different sports (Global Football, Running, Tennis, Snowboarding, and American Football). For each sport, custom data visualizations were needed showing current climate conditions' impacts on an athlete's ability to safely practice and play, plus future projections of impact should climate severity continue without intervention.

## Consolidated Narrative

For the 2019 Climate Summit in NYC, my team at Pitch Interactive collaborated with Rhodium Group on an interactive data visualization experience for Nike about current climate conditions and their impact on sports around the world. Nike's Social Impact team initiated the project, and Rhodium Group supplied the research for us to visualize. The project, titled "Move to Zero: Climate and Sport," focused on five different sports: Global Football, Running, Tennis, Snowboarding, and American Football.

At Pitch, the team included two developers (myself and a colleague), an executive creative director, and a project manager. I was responsible for creating the front-end layout and scrolling updates, along with shared responsibility for the individual visualizations for each sport with another developer. My specific responsibilities included scroll-linked animations of the charts and data values, as well as creating the lines and histograms visualizations.

The primary audience the general public, especially those interested in athletics and sport. Nike hoped to reach this audience with a compelling narrative about climate change's impact on sports.

Our design process focused on clearly highlighting projected climate impacts on each selected sport from climate change. We chose distinct chart types for each sport to make the data accessible and engaging. For example, for snowboarding we mapped the number of sub-freezing days in key mountain ranges, while for American football we created choropleth maps showing regions where game days are already being cancelled for heat and where changes to climate policy could reduce those impacts. We accompanied each visualization with quotes from prominent athletes in that sport to add human perspective and emotional resonance.

We also designed the experience to ensure a clear, easily readable narrative for each sport, even while scaling the designs down to smaller screens without flattening the rich underlying datasets. This required careful attention to typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy throughout the experience.

On the technical side, I focused on maintaining consistently smooth scroll-linked animations across multiple charts on mobile and desktop platforms. I implemented scrollama for scroll-linked animations, used React-Spring for smooth and performant animations, and carefully managed scroll events to maintain 60fps performance.

The final deliverable was a narrative experience that effectively illustrated the current and likely effects of climate change on athletes around the world. Users could scroll on mobile or desktop to trigger animations, interactions, and to guide them through the entire narrative. The experience provided both current climate condition impacts on an athlete's ability to safely practice and play their sport, as well as future projections of impact should climate severity continue without intervention. This compelling visualization helped raise awareness about climate change's tangible effects on the world of sport and inspired action toward sustainable solutions.
